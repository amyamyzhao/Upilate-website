Buyer Quality Planning

How to Compare Grip Coverage, Fit & Wash Durability

Method worksheet · Preview dated August 31, 2026

Use one documented method across samples, then separate what is visible from what has actually been tested.

Review the Leopard Ruffle Ankle product direction
Quick answer

Grip quality cannot be judged by dot count alone. A buyer should compare grip coverage, sock movement on the foot, size consistency, fabric recovery and changes after repeated care cycles. The supplier should state the test method, care conditions and acceptance criteria so that durability claims are tied to evidence.

Product photos can confirm visible details such as grip shape, color and approximate coverage. They cannot prove slip resistance, wash life, shrinkage, colorfastness or absolute safety.

Before comparing samples, agree one test method and one record sheet. The goal is not to create a marketing claim first; it is to decide what evidence the buyer needs before approving production.

1 · Visual review

Appearance is a starting point—not a quality test.

Photographs help identify construction and possible comparison points. Record the observations as visible facts, then verify material and performance on the approved sample.

Visible in a photo

Record what can be seen.

  • Grip shape, color and approximate distribution
  • Sock height, cuff shape and visible seams
  • Print, knit or color-block direction
  • Obvious gaps, misalignment or surface defects
Requires a test

Do not convert appearance into a claim.

  • Grip performance on a defined surface
  • Movement during a defined activity
  • Dimensional change after a defined care cycle
  • Colorfastness, recovery or durability result

2 · Coverage map

Compare grip by foot zone, not only by total dot count.

Use the same foot outline or zone definitions for every sample. Fill the decision column only after the buyer sets acceptance criteria.

3 · Fit checks

Use the same fit language across every size.

UPILATE’s current women’s structure uses S/M/L across the overall Chinese shoe-size 34–40 equivalent. The precise S/M/L conversion must follow the approved production size chart.

Relaxed lengthRecord the measurement points, unit and result before wear and after each agreed care checkpoint
Relaxed widthUse the same location and tension-free method across all samples
Heel positionRecord whether the heel panel sits correctly at the start and after the defined movement
Cuff comfortUse buyer-defined language and time period; avoid an absolute comfort claim
Compression or holdRecord the agreed method and observation; do not infer a numerical compression level
Sock movementRecord the activity, duration, direction and location of any movement

Define the wash-cycle method before recording a result.

6 · Evidence pack

Keep the test result connected to the approved sample.

Store the sample ID, dated photos, raw measurements, method, care conditions, deviations and reviewer decision together. If a result applies only to one construction or material, do not present it as a program-wide conclusion.

The current OEKO-TEX material is at yarn-supplier or yarn level and must not be described as finished-sock certification. A finished-product GRS scope is not confirmed.

Bulk approval gate

Close the specification before production starts.

Approve the physical sample, size chart, material composition, logo and sole artwork, grip material, color references, packaging, test method and acceptance criteria. Then confirm the quantity breakdown and production schedule.

For the photographed Leopard Ruffle Ankle direction, the visible print and heart grips are usable references; exact composition and test performance remain approval items.
