How to Approve a Custom Cuff Logo on Pilates Grip Socks

Artwork and sample guide · September 9, 2026

Turn the visible “LOGO” position into a traceable brief covering artwork, method, placement, dimensions, color, MOQ and physical-sample approval.

View the Custom Cuff-Logo Navy Crew

Quick answer

Approve a custom cuff logo by confirming the artwork, logo method, placement, finished size, thread color and contrast on a physical sock sample. Check readability on the ribbed cuff in both relaxed and worn conditions, then sign off the approved sample before bulk production. MOQ depends on the confirmed logo method and order structure.

A logo-position image is useful because it makes the intended branding area visible. It is still the beginning of the production brief—not evidence that a specific artwork, size, method or thread color has already been approved.

Keep the image as a customization cue, then connect every production decision to an artwork file, quotation, physical sample and named approver.

1 · Read the image correctly

“LOGO” shows the placement—not the finished branding.

This distinction lets the image communicate customization without pretending that the buyer artwork or production result already exists.

3 · Logo method

Choose the production route after the artwork and cuff are reviewed.

The MOQ below is confirmed project policy. The final method still depends on the selected construction, artwork and physical sample.

Knitted / jacquard direction

Plan from 50 pairs.

Jacquard-logo planning starts from 50 pairs per logo, color and size. Review small details, finished logo size, color contrast and how the design sits within the rib-knit cuff.

Embroidery direction

Plan from 100 pairs.

Embroidery planning starts from 100 pairs per logo, color and size when the cuff construction is suitable. Check stitch coverage, inside finish, hand feel, alignment and bulk consistency on the approved sample.

Order structure

Count every combination.

MOQ is calculated for one logo × one sock color × one size. Do not promise free mixing across colors or sizes unless the quotation explicitly confirms it.

Sample control

Keep one approved reference.

Record the artwork version, method, dimensions, thread reference, placement and dated sample. The approved record should control bulk production and future reorders.

4 · Physical sample

Review the logo in the states buyers will actually see.

A screenshot can approve direction. Only the physical sample can confirm the produced appearance on the ribbed cuff.

Cuff-logo sample checklist

Record the result against one sample ID.

Visual approval does not prove wash durability or performance. Those claims need a defined method and actual result.

  1. Artwork filename and version
  2. Logo method
  3. Finished width and height
  4. Cuff edge clearance
  5. Thread color reference
  6. Contrast against sock color
  7. Relaxed-cuff alignment
  8. Worn-cuff readability
  9. Inside finish / hand feel
  10. Pass / Revise / Pending
  11. Approved sample ID
  12. Named approval owner
