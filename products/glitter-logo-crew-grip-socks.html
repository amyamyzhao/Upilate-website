Custom Grip Sock FAQ

Answers for private-label and wholesale buyers.

Use these answers to prepare a clearer custom Pilates grip socks inquiry. The final quotation and approved sample control the order.

Jacquard logo production starts from 50 pairs. Embroidery production starts from 100 pairs. The MOQ is calculated separately for each logo, and the confirmed MOQ uses one color and one size unless the quotation states otherwise.

A ready-item quality sample has no MOQ and can be supplied free of product charge, with shipping paid by the buyer; preparation is normally one day. A custom-logo proofing sample has no MOQ but has a proofing cost, normally takes about seven days after the deposit is received, and the proofing fee can be credited against the bulk order.

The confirmed basis is one color and one size for each MOQ. Send the complete color and size plan so any alternative breakdown can be reviewed before quotation.

Gold or silver metallic yarn can be knitted into the sock. Rhinestone color and arrangement can be customized. Full-sole grip can use standard rubber or premium silicone, with custom patterns, colors and selected two-color grip directions available after confirmation.

Custom-logo sampling is counted after the deposit is received. Bulk timing is counted after the deposit and sample are both confirmed. The current reference is about seven days for custom proofing and about 20 days for bulk production, subject to the final order.

The currently discussed OEKO-TEX document relates to the yarn supplier. Buyers should request the applicable document and confirm whether its scope covers the selected yarn, factory and finished product before using a certification claim.
