Leopard Ruffle Ankle Grip Sock FAQ

Answers for sampling and wholesale planning.

The final quotation, artwork and approved physical sample control the order.

The standard direction is a women’s S/M/L structure covering the Chinese shoe-size 34–40 equivalent. The exact S/M/L conversion should follow the approved production size chart rather than an invented public mapping.

A knitted jacquard logo starts from 50 pairs and an embroidered logo starts from 100 pairs, calculated per logo, color and size. The final method follows artwork and sample review.

Full-sole grip can use standard rubber or premium silicone. Choose one of our grip patterns or submit your own pattern for production review. Standard grip is normally single-color, while selected two-color directions are also supported.

A ready-item quality sample has no MOQ and is free of product charge, with shipping paid by the buyer; preparation is normally about one day. A custom-logo sample has no MOQ but carries a proofing fee, normally takes about seven days after deposit, and the fee can be credited against the bulk order.

The current reference is about 20 days after both the deposit and sample are confirmed. The final timing follows the approved order, artwork and production schedule.

The currently discussed OEKO-TEX document is at yarn-supplier or yarn level. It should not be presented as a finished-sock certification unless the applicable scope is separately verified.
