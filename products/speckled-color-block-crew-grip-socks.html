Speckled Crew FAQ

Answers for colorway, sampling and wholesale planning.

The final quotation, artwork and approved physical sample control the order.

The supplied images show a cream speckled ribbed crew body, a blue-and-brown color-block cuff and blue sole grips. Repeat-order color consistency and available production colorways should be confirmed against the approved sample and production record.

The standard direction is women’s S/M/L covering the Chinese shoe-size 34–40 equivalent. The exact S/M/L conversion follows the approved production size chart and should not be invented from the marketing image.

Knitted jacquard and embroidery are available program directions, with MOQ and feasibility depending on the selected method. The exact process used for the photographed heart detail should be confirmed before it is published as a product specification.

A knitted jacquard logo starts from 50 pairs and an embroidered logo starts from 100 pairs, calculated per logo, color and size. A custom sole mold starts from 100 pairs plus a mold fee; standard grip does not add a separate grip MOQ.

A ready-item quality sample has no MOQ and is free of product charge, with shipping paid by the buyer; preparation is normally about one day. A custom-logo sample has no MOQ but carries a proofing fee, normally takes about seven days after deposit, and the fee can be credited against the bulk order.

A free OPP bag is the standard option. Paper sleeves, header cards, barcodes, boxes and custom packaging are supported, with material, dimensions, printing, MOQ, fees and sampling time confirmed for each project.
