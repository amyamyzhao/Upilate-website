Planning Inputs

What helps us shape a useful quotation.

You do not need a complete specification sheet on day one. A few commercial facts are enough to create a strong starting brief.

SpaceRoom dimensions, desired class capacity and whether equipment needs to move or store.
UsersStudio, gym, rehabilitation, hospitality, private training or resale market.
EquipmentPreferred reformer type, number of units and supporting chair, barrel, box or aerial products.
DeliveryDestination country, target date and preferred FOB, CIF or DDP discussion.

Three Ways to Buy

Different buyers need different quotation logic.

Tell us where you sit in the market so the product, packaging and commercial discussion are organized for the right use case.

Studio operators

Prioritize training experience, room capacity, coordinated finishes and practical accessory coverage.

Gyms & projects

Prioritize consistent configuration, delivery planning, documentation and multi-unit order clarity.

Distributors

Prioritize model range, repeat ordering, private label, packaging and market-specific assortment planning.
